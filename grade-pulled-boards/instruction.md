# Grade forty boards of sawn oak

Produce `/app/grades.csv`: the header `board,grade` and one line for each of the
forty boards listed in `/app/to_grade.csv`, giving that board's grade under the
mill's works standard, spelled as the standard spells it. Thirty six of the
forty must be right.

`/app/GRADING.md` is the works standard. It gives the grades and the yield each
one requires, how a board's cuttings are laid out and the least cutting that
counts, how the wane comes off, and that a board takes the grade of its poorer
face.

`/app/boards.csv` is the stock book: every board in the shed, with the parcel it
belongs to, the bench it was sawn at, whether it was measured green or dry, and
its length, width and thickness in millimetres.

`/app/defects.csv` is the defect book: a line for every knot and every shake,
giving the board, the face it is on, where its centre lies along and across the
board, and its size along and across.

`/app/wane.csv` gives the wane by board and edge. `/app/parcels.csv` gives each
parcel and what it was cut for.

You have 14400 seconds to complete this task. Do not cheat by using online solutions or hints specific to this task.
